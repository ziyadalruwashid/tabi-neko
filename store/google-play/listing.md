# Google Play store listing

Copy each box into the Play Console: **Grow users → Store presence → Main
store listing**. English is the default language. Add Arabic with
**Manage translations → Add your own translation text → Arabic (ar)**.

Character limits are Google's. Every text below fits; the counts are in
brackets.

---

## English (default)

**App name** (25 of 30)
```
Tabi Neko: Japan Notebook
```

**Short description** (76 of 80)
```
Plan Japan day by day with Apollo the cat: 498 places, halal food and stamps
```

**Full description** (under 4,000)
```
Tabi Neko is a travel notebook for Japan, written by someone who walked it. Apollo the cat is your guide: he picks the places and answers your questions, like a friend who has already been.

🗺️ 498 PLACES, ALL IN ONE MAP
Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Kobe, Nara, Kamakura and Ise. Coffee shops, ramen, temples, shrines, views, markets, vintage shops and hidden gems, each with a tip, a price and how long to spend there. See what's near you and what's open now.

🗓️ PLAN YOUR TRIP DAY BY DAY
Add places to your trip and drop them into days, with your flights and hotels in one place. Your trip is saved to your account, so it's the same on every phone.

🐱 ASK APOLLO
Ask anything about Japan: where to eat, how to get there, what to do on a rainy day. Apollo answers from his own notebook, shows the places as cards you can add to your trip, and can turn an answer into a day plan in one tap.

🍜 HALAL-FRIENDLY FOOD
78 halal and Muslim-friendly places, marked clearly. Halal Lens reads a food label or a menu on your phone, even without internet, and points out pork, alcohol and ingredients to ask about. It's a helper, not a halal certificate, and big Japanese questions to show the staff are one tap away.

🐾 COLLECT STAMPS
Visit a famous spot and collect its stamp in your stamp book, just like the real stamp books in Japan.

🚄 DAY TRIPS
15 day trips from Tokyo and from Kyoto & Osaka, shown by how far they are by train, with the pass, the price, the halal food and what not to miss.

📱 MORE FOR THE ROAD
• Areas: what each neighbourhood is like and who it's for
• Apps to download before you fly, picked by Apollo
• Trip reminders and the weather for your days
• Your trip as a PDF to share or print
• English and Arabic

🔒 YOUR TRIP IS YOURS
No ads. Your data is never sold. No tracking. Exploring needs no account; you only log in to plan trips and collect stamps, and you can delete your account at any time.

Pick your travel buddy: Apollo the cat, Kenbo the eagle, Fa the duck, Lily the fennec or Sloom the red panda. Mata ne! またね
```
